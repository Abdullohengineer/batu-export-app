# Correct KN pallet rounding on 190826-001/002, split one pallet to fix Sep 10 output total

## What changed

Two related, operator-confirmed corrections to KN (Konditerka) pallets on
serials 190826-001 (P10-S) and 190826-002 (P11). Applied together in one
transaction, archived at
`docs/data-corrections/2026-09-15_190826-001-002-kn-rounding-and-sep10-split.sql`.

**Correction 1 — 10kg-box rounding.** KN is packed in 10kg boxes, so
pallet quantities must be multiples of 10. `PLT-190826-001-KN-2` was
1,997 kg (invalid) → corrected to 2,000 kg. `PLT-190826-002-KN-1` was
2,573 kg — not left at the naively-rounded 2,570, see Correction 2.

**Correction 2 — Sep 10 factory-wide KN output total.** Operator specified
the real total KN output on 2026-09-10 (across all serials) must be
exactly 2,260 kg, with the remainder able to land on a later date. Before
this correction, only `PLT-190826-001-KN-2` (2,000 kg after Correction 1)
was dated 2026-09-10 — 260 kg short.

Confirmed with the operator before touching anything: this was a real
physical event (part of one pallet's contents genuinely came out
2026-09-10, the rest 2026-09-12) that had been logged as a single pallet
entry dated 2026-09-12. Fixed by splitting `PLT-190826-002-KN-1` into two
records for the **same** serial (190826-002) — the only mechanism
considered that doesn't mean attributing one wash cycle's physical output
to a different serial's, which would be fabrication, not correction:

- `PLT-190826-002-KN-1`: reduced to 2,310 kg, stays dated 2026-09-12 ("the
  rest, later").
- `PLT-190826-002-KN-3` (new): 260 kg, dated 2026-09-10. No pre-existing
  physical barcode for this split portion — operator confirmed a synthetic
  new barcode is acceptable.

2,310 + 260 = 2,570 — the Correction-1-rounded total for this pallet is
unchanged by the split, so serial 190826-002's own aggregate KN output
(and therefore its own computed loss) is unaffected by Correction 2.

**No Kalibr (K1-K8) pallets touched.** Confirmed live before applying:
`PLT-190826-001-02-1` (Kalibr 2, 250 kg, dated 2026-09-10 — the operator's
own reference point for "shouldn't change") is untouched; this correction
only ever names KN barcodes.

## Why a split, not a reassignment

Considered and rejected: moving weight between `PLT-190826-001-KN-2`
(serial 190826-001) and `PLT-190826-002-KN-1` (serial 190826-002) to hit
2,260 on Sep 10 without any new row. The arithmetic works (2000+260=2260,
2570-260=2310), but the two pallets belong to **different serials** — each
serial's KN pallets are physically only the output of that serial's own
wash cycle. Redistributing between them would mean claiming one serial's
physical output as another's, which is fabrication regardless of how
clean the numbers look. Confirmed with the operator this was indeed the
190826-002-KN-1 pallet's own contents splitting across two logging dates,
not a cross-serial reallocation.

## Verification

Dry-run (`BEGIN...ROLLBACK`) before applying, using `client_serial_loss_kg`
— the app's own canonical loss function, not an approximation — both
before and after:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 190826-001 (P10-S) loss | 688 kg | **685 kg** |
| 190826-002 (P11) loss | 689 kg | **692 kg** (7 kg apart, as expected) |
| Sep 10 KN total (factory-wide) | 2,000 kg | **2,260 kg** |
| Sep 10 Kalibr-2 total | 250 kg | 250 kg (unchanged) |

Applied for real after the dry-run matched exactly. Live re-verification
post-apply confirmed identical figures. Three paired `audit_log` entries
(ids 1368, 1369, 1370) — two updates, one insert (before: `null`) — cover
every row touched.
