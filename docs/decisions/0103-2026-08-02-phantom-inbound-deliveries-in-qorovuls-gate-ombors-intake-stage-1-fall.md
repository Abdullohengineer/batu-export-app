## 2026-08-02 — Phantom inbound deliveries in Qorovul's gate + Ombor's intake (Stage 1 fallout, shipped separately)

**Context.** Found while tracing where a Stage 3 minted order would land, not by a bug report. Neither `useKirimTrips` (Qorovul's KIRIM gate) nor `useIntakeLines` (Ombor's Skladga KIRIM) filtered `kirim_orders.origin`, so the 5 opening-stock anchor orders Stage 1 seeded appeared as real inbound work: Qorovul saw **5 phantom trucks queued to be weighed** (`status='kutilmoqda'` + no `gate_weighings` row is literally that screen's "not started" predicate) and Ombor saw **5 phantom deliveries queued to be accepted** (no `storage_intake` row is literally its "pending" predicate). Confirmed from data plus the filter predicates before touching anything.

**Why this was worse than the four earlier leaks.** Those distorted *reports*. These were *action* screens with a destructive action available: accepting one would have written a real `storage_intake` row and materialised **~52 tonnes of phantom raw stock** in qoldig'i. Stage 1 deliberately gave old-washed serials no `storage_intake` (correct for the balance maths) — which is exactly what makes them look like un-processed inbound deliveries to the two roles whose whole job is processing inbound deliveries.

**Fix.** `.eq('origin','delivery')` on both hooks — a positive allowlist, so Stage 3's `internal_reprocess` origin is covered by construction rather than needing a second exclusion later. Shipped as its own branch/PR ahead of Stage 3 at the user's explicit instruction, since it was visible to staff in production. Verified live: Qorovul reads "Kutilmoqda 0 / Faol reys yo'q", Ombor reads "Kutilayotgan reys yo'q", and every genuine delivery still shows in both.
